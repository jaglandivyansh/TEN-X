How to use

  1. Place the phone at chest height, 2 to 3 metres away, side-on or front-on.
  2. Keep your shooting arm and both shoulders in view, with good light.
  3. Shot routine mode: breathe in and out to relax, breathe in while raising the arm, breathe out and settle to aim, press, then keep the arm up for the follow-through. Use dry-fire (unloaded) only and follow your range's safety rules. A hold of 6 to 8 seconds suits one breath.
  4. Optional: turn the camera on, raise your arm straight and tap Calibrate. The app then fits the straight-arm angle to you.
  5. Press Start session. Hold when you hear the high beep, relax on the low beep.
What the scores mean
  • In zone: share of the hold your elbow was straight.
  • Steady: 0 to 100, higher means less wrist wobble.
  • Height: “Dropped” means your arm sank during the hold.
  • Sway: body movement side to side.

It checks posture only, not sights or the pistol. Stop if your arm or shoulder hurts.
